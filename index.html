How to play

Your next move starts here.

  1. Choose a piece

    Look at the available blocks and find an open space on the board that matches their shape.

  2. Complete a line

    Drag a piece into position. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and earn points.

  3. Think ahead

    Keep enough room for future pieces. The game ends when none of the available pieces can fit.
